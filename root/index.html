About

I am a Software Engineer at Thermo Fisher Scientific and a graduate student at Portland State University. I have been working as a professional for 8 years. Coming out of my undergraduate studies, I wasn't sure what I really loved to do. However, since working at Thermo Fisher I have found a passion for designing quality software architecture, solving computer vision problems, and collaborating with hardware and systems engineers.
